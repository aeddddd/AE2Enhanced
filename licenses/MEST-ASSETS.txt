src/main/resources/assets/ae2enhanced/textures/gui/dock/background.png
    Derived from MEST's
    src/main/resources/assets/mesplicedterminal/textures/guis/network_tool_panel.png
    (MIT, same upstream/copyright as above).

    Derivation (performed once, reproducibly):
      * middle 248x248 area, top edge band, bottom edge band, right edge band and the
        top-right / bottom-right corners are copied verbatim from the source texture;
      * MEST's source art is drawn for a left-flush section (its columns 0..3 and its
        top-left / bottom-left corners are fully transparent), so the left edge band and
        the two left corners are produced by mirroring the source's right band and right
        corners horizontally. No colour outside the source texture is introduced;
      * the result is a symmetric 256x256 nine-slice with a 4px border and a 248x248
        tileable interior, i.e. the layout MEST's ModulePanel.drawGeneratedBackground
        and its Blitter("guis/background.png", 256, 256) expect.

    Reason: MEST's `BG` blitter points at AE2's own window background
    (assets/ae2/textures/guis/background.png, 256x256, 4px border), which does not exist
    in Applied Energistics 2 for Minecraft 1.12.2, and MEST itself ships no such file.
    The 1.12.2 rewrite therefore needs its own copy of that nine-slice artwork.

    textures/guis/extra_panels.png        (not present upstream either)
